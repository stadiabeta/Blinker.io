- - - - THE BASICS - - - -
This game is very simple on the dashboard you have three buttons. Choose the one to your liking.
You have a settings button next to the game title. Click it to view your stats or the game version.
When in Blinker Mode, A New Stop Blinker button will appear for the peoples who already blinked!

- - - - LEVELING - - - -
The leveling system is balanced with a 20 second cooldown for whatever hit you take on each one. You can
take a hit then hit a blinker and get xp. But cannot spam the hit for XP. You get 20 XP for a single Hit and 200 for a Blinker.
The XP Given increases as you level up.

- - - - MORE OF A BUD SMOKER? - - - -
If you're more of a bud smoker, Sadly we havent added the gates to those halls yet. But you surely can use the take a hit
and randomized hit buttons for just joints!
